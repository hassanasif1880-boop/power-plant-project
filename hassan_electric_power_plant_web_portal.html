ECO-RESPONSIBILITY

DECARBONIZATION & CLEAN VISION

Hassan Energy is committed to reaching net-zero operational carbon emissions by 2038 through tech innovations.

Roadmap to Net-Zero Power Generation

Our environmental charter sets stringent benchmarks for carbon intensity reduction, watershed protection around hydroelectric reservoirs, and zero-waste cooling systems.

Carbon Capture & Sequestration (CCS)

Capturing over 85% of flue-gas carbon dioxide from natural gas units for synthetic fuel synthesis and deep geological storage.

Grid Battery Buffer (500 MWh)

Large-scale lithium-iron-phosphate battery energy storage systems smooth solar duck-curve volatility.

Zero Water Waste

Closed-loop cooling towers save over 12 million cubic meters of natural water resources annually.

Biodiversity Zones

1,500 hectares of protected wetlands surrounding our hydro dam dedicated to native wildlife habitats.

99% By-Product Reuse

All combustion byproducts are safely captured and repurposed for eco-friendly construction materials.
